Prism Spire: notice
===================

Prism Spire is an original pass-the-remote party game of stacking glass prisms, running natively
on Fire TV's Vega OS (C, SDL3, OpenGL ES and Box2D). Its sound and music are synthesized live by
the game; it ships no recorded audio.

Source code
-----------
The complete source code for this build is at https://github.com/mitulshah3882/firetv-native
(directory apps/prism-spire). Third-party sources are not stored in that repository:
tools/fetch-deps.sh downloads them at the exact versions listed below.
